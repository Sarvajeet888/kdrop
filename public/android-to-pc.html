K-Drop · Send files from Android to a computer

Send files from Android to a computer

Android Chrome handles this particularly well — files stream straight to disk on the receiving computer, so size is rarely a problem.

Open K-Drop See the steps

Step by step

  1. Open K-Drop on the computer.
  2. On Android, open the camera app and point it at the QR code, then tap the link.
  3. Tap Choose files and select from your gallery or file manager.
  4. Accept on the computer and the transfer starts.

Worth knowing

Large videos are fine here

Chrome and Edge write the incoming file to disk as it arrives rather than holding it in memory, so a multi-gigabyte video works.

Keep the screen awake

If the phone sleeps mid-transfer, Android may suspend the page. For a long transfer, keep the screen on.

Original quality

Files are not recompressed. What you send is exactly what arrives, verified on the other end.

Questions

Do I need to install anything from the Play Store?

No. It runs in Chrome. You can add it to your home screen if you want, but that installs nothing.

Can I send a whole album?

Yes. Select multiple files, or choose a folder.

Does it work with Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus?

Yes, any Android phone with a current browser.

How it works underneath

K-Drop opens a connection directly between the two browsers. Our server introduces them to each other and then stops being involved — it never sees the file. When a network blocks a direct connection, the file is encrypted on your own device with a key derived from your PIN, which we never receive, and passed through our relay as data we cannot read. Nothing is written to disk on our side.

Full detail is in the documentation.
