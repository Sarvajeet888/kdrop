K-Drop · Send files from your phone to your PC

Send files from your phone to your PC

No cable, no app to install, and nothing uploaded to a cloud drive. Open the same page on both, match the code, and the files move directly between them.

Open K-Drop See the steps

Step by step

  1. On your computer, open K-Drop. A six-character code and a four-character PIN appear.
  2. On your phone, point the camera at the QR code and open the link — or type the code and PIN by hand.
  3. The computer shows up in your phone’s device list.
  4. On the phone, tap Choose files and pick your photos or documents.
  5. Accept the transfer on the computer. The files save straight to disk.

Worth knowing

Photos keep their original quality

Nothing is recompressed on the way through. A 48-megapixel photo arrives as the same file that left, byte for byte — unlike most messaging apps.

Works without Wi-Fi sharing

Same Wi-Fi is fastest. If the phone is on mobile data and the computer on Wi-Fi it still works, just more slowly.

iPhone users

Safari holds the incoming file in memory, so very large videos can fail. Sending from the iPhone is more reliable than receiving on it.

Questions

Do I need a USB cable?

No. The transfer happens over the network between the two browsers.

Does this work with an iPhone and a Windows PC?

Yes. Both run a browser, and that is all K-Drop needs.

Are the photos uploaded anywhere?

On the direct route they never reach our server. If a network blocks that route, they are encrypted on your phone before passing through our relay, which cannot read them.

How it works underneath

K-Drop opens a connection directly between the two browsers. Our server introduces them to each other and then stops being involved — it never sees the file. When a network blocks a direct connection, the file is encrypted on your own device with a key derived from your PIN, which we never receive, and passed through our relay as data we cannot read. Nothing is written to disk on our side.

Full detail is in the documentation.
