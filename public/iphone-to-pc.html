K-Drop · Send files from an iPhone to a computer

Send files from an iPhone to a computer

AirDrop only talks to Apple devices. This works to any computer, including Windows, using only Safari.

Open K-Drop See the steps

Step by step

  1. Open K-Drop on the computer.
  2. On the iPhone, open the Camera app and point it at the QR code, then tap the banner.
  3. Tap Choose files and pick from your photo library.
  4. Accept on the computer. The files save to disk there.

Worth knowing

Sending works better than receiving

Safari has no way to stream an incoming file to disk, so it holds the whole thing in memory. Sending from the iPhone avoids that limit entirely.

HEIC photos stay HEIC

The file is sent unchanged. If you need JPEG, convert before sending or change the iPhone camera setting to "Most Compatible".

Do not background the app

Switching apps or taking a call can suspend Safari and stop the transfer. Keep the screen on until it finishes.

Questions

Do I need iTunes?

No. Nothing to install on either device.

Why not just use AirDrop?

AirDrop only works between Apple devices. This works to Windows, Android and Linux too.

Will my photos lose quality?

No. The file arrives byte-for-byte identical and is checked on arrival.

How it works underneath

K-Drop opens a connection directly between the two browsers. Our server introduces them to each other and then stops being involved — it never sees the file. When a network blocks a direct connection, the file is encrypted on your own device with a key derived from your PIN, which we never receive, and passed through our relay as data we cannot read. Nothing is written to disk on our side.

Full detail is in the documentation.
